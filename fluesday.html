Things According to Last Fluesday

2023  | Sculpture Device

Description:

All we’re allowed of Last Fluesday are the auditory and visual entrapments within this box, when it was last allowed to absorb the environment as it was; for it was then sealed with helical fasteners turned by a long and skinny rotator quite visibly angry.

Notes and extras:

This device broadcasts the "sound" within itslef (presumably silence) over FM radio. You can "tune into" last Fluesday on 94.7 FM if you're in close proximity.

Inspired by Duchamp's With Hidden Noise (1916) and 50cc of Paris Air (1919).
