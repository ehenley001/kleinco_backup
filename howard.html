Howard Time System

The Howard Time System (HTS) reinterprets the traditional base-60 (sexagesimal) system of time measurement into 70-minute "Howards".

There are...

  • 1 hour 10 minutes in a Howard
  • 6.86 Howards in an 8-hour work shift
  • 10.29 Howards on a 12-hour clock face
  • 20.57 Howards in a Day
