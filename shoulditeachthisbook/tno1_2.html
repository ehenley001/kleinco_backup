Do not teach this book yet.

You will need to spend more than one day exploring critical perspectives of the text with your students. Part of this process should include time for you and your students to make connections between this book and contemporary issues around race and ethnicity that are unique to your community and context. Disrupt Texts provides alternatives and critiques of key canonical texts.
