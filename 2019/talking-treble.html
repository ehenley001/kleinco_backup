YouTube

At sporadic points throughout the years of 2015 - Present, Alexander Harding and Eli Henley -- in recent collaboration with some other friends -- have live-streamed the music/variety talk show Talking Treble (formerly Talkin' Treble) on YouTube.  The first 3 episodes showcased a loose strict-talk format, which proved terribly bland.  Episode 4 saw a restructuring, one that valued live performance of all types in addition to talk -- an "open mic night" style.

 

There is no true schedule to the streams, but more are sure to come.
